About MadiBets

Welcome to MadiBets! 🎓🏆

MadiBets is a gamified prediction and engagement platform built specifically for the university community. We've taken the thrill and friendly rivalry of sports betting and reimagined it for campus life; bringing students, lecturers, and faculty closer together through shared experiences, friendly competition, and academic motivation.

Our Mission

University life is about more than just lectures; it's about the campus atmosphere, varsity sports, student culture, and academic milestones. MadiBets was created to boost student participation and community spirit across all aspects of university life.

Whether you're cheering on your campus sports teams, staying engaged with academic pass rates, or keeping track of major campus events, MadiBets turns everyday university moments into an exciting, competitive experience.

How It Works

  • 100% Risk-Free & Gamified: MadiBets operates strictly as a virtual, non-monetary platform. There is no real-world money involved, and no real-world risk.
  • Monthly MadiBucks: Every registered student receives a monthly allowance of 100 MadiBucks—our custom digital currency—to participate in prediction markets.
  • Academic & Campus Markets: Place your predictions on sports matches, varsity events, social happenings, or academic outcomes.
  • Student-Proposed Predictions: Got a great idea for a prediction? Students can submit their own custom market proposals to administrators for approval and custom odds.
  • Lecturer Tasks: Earn extra MadiBucks by completing module-specific tasks set by your lecturers, giving you a fun boost to keep up with your studies!
  • Climb the Leaderboards: Form groups, add friends, and track your performance against peers across academic faculties and leagues.

Key Features at a Glance

Feature Description
MadiBucks Currency Free monthly allowance used exclusively for placing predictions.
Prediction Categories Academic, Sports, and Social prediction markets.
Leaderboards & Leagues Track your success rate, win streaks, and net MadiBucks balance against friends and faculty.
Study Motivation Earn additional virtual currency by engaging with study tasks provided by lecturers.
Community Driven Propose predictions, form study or social groups, and build your friend network.
